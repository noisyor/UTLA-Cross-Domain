The research question

What if the profiling device looks nothing like the target?

Prior unsupervised attacks look for one domain-invariant representation. That assumption works when source and target are similar, but becomes a bottleneck as the discrepancy grows.

UTLA keeps the classification task shared and lets each domain use its own encoder.

Three sources of discrepancy

One method across changing attack surfaces.

UTLA is evaluated across the changes that make direct model transfer difficult in practice.
