The UTLA approach

One classifier.
Two encoders.

UTLA freezes the source classifier, trains a target-specific encoder adversarially, and adds explicit multi-layer MMD regularization to stabilize alignment under large domain gaps.

Three stages

Profile, adapt, and recover.

The target labels remain unavailable throughout adaptation and hyperparameter selection.

01

Profile the source

Train source encoder Ep and classifier Cp using labeled traces collected from a controlled source device.

02

Adapt the target

Learn target encoder Et from unlabeled target traces using adversarial training and multi-layer MMD.

03

Recover the key

Pair Et with the frozen Cp, rank key hypotheses, and recover the unknown target key.

Target encoder objective

Adversarial alignment plus explicit distribution matching.

ℒ*advE = ℒadvE + Σ λi · MMD²(Fp,i, Ft,i)

MMD is applied at multiple encoder layers. The paper uses λ1 = 2 at the encoder output and λ2 = 0.05 at the penultimate layer.

Why separate encoders?

Alignment without forcing identical representations.

A single common encoder can collapse under substantial domain shift. Separate source and target encoders lower shared-task error while preserving one classifier.
